# 🍽️ **Food Cycle**

**Food Cycle** is a Flutter application that connects **Needy people** with **Donators** to reduce food waste and fight hunger.  
Donators can list **surplus food**, and needy users can find **nearby donations** and contact the donators directly — all through a **real-time chat interface**.

---

## ✨ **Features**

👥 **Two User Roles:**

- **Needy**: Browse donations, view nearby food listings on a map, and receive food.
- **Donator**: Upload food donations with descriptions, location, and categories.

🧭 **Google Maps Integration:**

- See the **exact food pickup location**.

💬 **Real-Time Chat:**

- **Seamless communication** between needy and donators.

📍 **Location-Based Filtering:**

- View donations **near you**.

🔍 **Search & Filter:**

- Search food items and **filter by category**.

⭐ **Rating System:**

- **Rate donators** based on your experience.

⚡ **Upload Donations:**

- Donators can **quickly upload new food listings** with photos.

🧠 **State Management:**

- Uses **Provider** for **easy and scalable state management**.
